Before you start

  • Everyone signs in with an account created by an administrator. You'll get an email with a link to set your own password.
  • Administrators find their pages in the Administration section of the left sidebar.
  • Teachers and students use their own portal menu in the sidebar (for example Utama, Bahan saya, Kelas saya).
  • Some labels in the app are in Malay, for example Padam = Delete, Batal = Cancel, Simpan = Save. These guides use the labels exactly as they appear on screen.
