Before you start

  • Sign in with an Administrator or Superadmin account. Other roles may not see the Administration menu.
  • All admin pages are under the Administration section of the left sidebar.
  • Some labels in the app are shown in Malay (for example Padam = Delete, Batal = Cancel, Simpan = Save). These guides use the labels exactly as they appear on screen.
