1. Your account

  1. An administrator creates your teacher (Guru) account. You can't sign up as a teacher yourself.
  2. You'll get an email with a link to set your password. Open it and choose a password.
  3. Verify your email address if asked, then sign in.
Forgot your password? Use the "forgot password" link on the sign-in page, or ask an administrator to send you a reset link. To change your password, go to your account settings.

3. Utama (overview)

  • Tambah bahan adds a new material.
  • Akan Luput lists materials about to expire. Click +1 bulan to extend one by a month.
  • Tiada Akses Bulan Ini lists students without access this month (their monthly fee hasn't been recorded). Only an administrator can change fee status.

4. Classes and sessions

Create a class

  1. Open Kelas saya and click Tambah kelas.
  2. Enter Nama kelas and Tingkatan (both required).
  3. Optionally choose Subjek, Laluan (pathway), Hari (day), and Masa mula / Masa tamat (start and end time).
  4. Click Simpan. You'll see "Kelas disimpan."

Each class row has Sunting (edit), Kelola pelajar (manage students) and Arkib (archive).

Schedule a session

  1. Click Jadual sesi.
  2. Choose the Kelas, then set Mula (start) and Tamat (end). The end must be after the start.
  3. Click save. You'll see "Sesi dijadualkan."
If the session overlaps another one of yours, you'll see a warning, but you can still book it.

Under Sesi akan datang (upcoming sessions), use Jadual semula to reschedule or Batal to cancel. Students are notified automatically.

5. Adding students to a class

  1. On the class row, click Kelola pelajar.
  2. Click Tambah pelajar and pick the student. Use Keluarkan to remove one.
You can add a student only if:
  • they have a Pelajar (student) account,
  • their fee for the current month has been recorded as paid, and
  • they aren't already in another teacher's class.
Student accounts are created by an administrator. Teachers can't create them.

6. Uploading materials

  1. Open Bahan saya and click Tambah bahan.
  2. Choose the type: PDF, Video, Question PDF or Quiz.
  3. Enter the Tajuk (title) and optionally a description.
  4. Choose the Subjek. Optionally pick one of your classes under Kelas, or leave it as "Semua kelas" (all classes).
  5. For a video, paste the Pautan video. For a PDF, upload the file under Fail PDF (maks 20MB).
  6. Set Bulan akses (the month the material belongs to) and Tarikh luput (expiry date). Quick buttons can set end of month, +1, +3 months or end of year.
  7. Click Simpan. You'll see "Bahan ditambah."
Who can see it: only students whose fee for that access month has been recorded as paid. Students can view and stream materials in the app but can't download them.

In the list, each material shows its access month, expiry, status (Aktif / Luput) and how many students can access it. Use +1 bulan to extend.

7. Building an exam

An exam is a Quiz material. It reaches the students in the chosen class (or all classes) who have paid for its access month, until it expires. There's no separate "assign" step.

  1. Create a material of type Quiz (see above), then click Bina ujian on it.
  2. Under Tetapan (settings), set:
    • Tempoh (minit): time limit, 1–600 minutes.
    • Dedah jawapan: show answers After Submission or After Exam Closes.
    • Maks percubaan: maximum attempts, 1–20.
    • Soalan rawak: shuffle questions.
    Click Simpan.
  3. Click Tambah bahagian to add a section. Enter a Kod (short code), Tajuk, and Jenis: MCQ, Subjektif, Pemahaman (comprehension, needs a passage) or Esei.
  4. Click Tambah soalan to add questions: enter the Soalan and Markah (1–100).
    • MCQ / Pemahaman: options A and B are required (up to F), plus Jawapan betul.
    • Esei: set a Sasaran patah perkataan (word target).
    • Optionally add a Skema jawapan (marking scheme).
    Or reuse questions with Dari bank soalan › choose › Salin.
  5. Use Semak jawapan to review and mark answers. MCQ answers are marked automatically. Subjective and essay answers wait for you to mark them.
Once any student has answered, the questions are locked. Only the settings can still be changed.
Scores: portal exams give a score but have no pass mark. Course quizzes (set up under Kursus) are different: they have a pass mark, 70% by default.

8. Calendar and Google Calendar

  1. Open Kalendar to see the week's sessions for all teachers. Use Minggu lepas / Minggu depan to change weeks and Tapis guru to filter by teacher.
  2. To put your sessions in Google Calendar, click Sambung Google Calendar and allow calendar access on Google's screen.
  3. Click Segerak sekarang to send your sessions. You can sync once every 2 minutes.
  4. To disconnect, click Putuskan. If access expires, click Sambung semula.
Calendar events contain a secure link into the app, not a raw Zoom link. Students join classes through the app.

9. Exam pathways

Open Kurikulum › Pembina laluan to create pathways and add subjects, chapters and lessons. See How to create a subject pathway. Linking plans to a pathway is done by an administrator.
