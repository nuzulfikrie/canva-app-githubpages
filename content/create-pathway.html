How to create a subject pathway

An exam pathway (for example SPM, STPM, UASA, UPKK or Matrikulasi) groups subjects, chapters, lessons and a question bank.

You need an Administrator, Superadmin or Guru role. Only Administrators and Superadmins can link plans.

1. Open Exam Pathways

  1. In the left sidebar, open Administration › Akademi › Pathways. Teachers (Guru) open Kurikulum › Pembina laluan instead.
  2. The Exam Pathways page lists each pathway with its number of subjects and questions.

2. Create the pathway

  1. Click New pathway. A window opens.
  2. Enter a Name (required), for example "SPM 2027".
  3. Optionally add a Description (up to 500 characters).
  4. Click Create. You'll see "Pathway created."

3. Open the pathway builder

  1. Click the pathway's name in the list (or … › Edit).
  2. The Pembina laluan (pathway builder) section opens: "Susun subjek, bab, dan pelajaran laluan ini."

4. Add subjects

Choose one of the following:

  • New subject: type the subject name in Tambah subjek (for example "Jawi") and click Tambah subjek. If a subject with that name already exists, it is reused.
  • Existing subject: pick one from Pautkan subjek sedia ada (link an existing subject).

The subject is linked to the pathway and its page opens.

5. Add chapters and lessons

  1. To add a chapter (bab), click + on the subject row or Tambah bab on the subject page.
  2. To add a lesson (pelajaran), click + on the chapter row or Tambah pelajaran on the chapter page. The new lesson opens so you can write its content.
  3. To rename, enter the new name in Nama baharu and click Simpan (or Batal to cancel).
  4. Use the up and down arrows to reorder chapters and lessons. Kembang semua expands everything and Kuncup semua collapses everything.
  5. To delete a chapter or lesson, type its name in the Padam window to confirm.

6. Link plans (optional)

  1. In the Pelan section, tick the plans that unlock this pathway.
  2. Click Simpan pelan. You'll see "Plans saved."
Leave all plans unticked to keep the pathway open to every student. Only active plans are listed. See How to create a plan.

7. Build the question bank (optional)

  1. In the Bank soalan section, tick the approved questions to include.
  2. Click Simpan bank soalan. You'll see "Question bank saved."
Only approved questions appear here. Approve questions in the Bank soalan area first.

Deleting a pathway

Choose … › Delete, type the pathway name exactly, and click Padam.

A pathway can't be deleted while students are still learning in it. You'll see "Laluan ini masih mempunyai pelajar yang sedang belajar."
