Pelajar / Student guide

How to sign in, view materials, join classes, take exams, watch recordings and track your progress.

1. Your account

  1. Your school or tuition centre administrator creates your student (Pelajar) account and places you in a class. Signing up on your own doesn't give you student access.
  2. You'll get an email with a link to set your password. Open it and choose a password.
  3. Verify your email address if asked, then sign in.
Forgot your password? Use the "forgot password" link on the sign-in page, or ask your administrator to send a reset link.

2. Monthly fees and access

Your monthly tuition fee is not paid in the app. Your administrator records each month's payment in Bayaran & Akses (payments & access).

  • A month recorded as paid unlocks that month's materials, class sessions and recordings.
  • If a month isn't recorded yet, you'll see "Yuran … belum dibayar" and that month's materials stay locked.
  • Questions about your fee status? Contact your administrator. Teachers can't change it.

3. Utama (home)

Open Utama to see your fee status for the year (Status Yuran), your latest materials (Bahan Terkini, marked Boleh akses or Dikunci), and active exams (Ujian Aktif).

4. Viewing materials

  1. Open Bahan saya.
  2. Filter with Semua (all), Dibayar (paid) or Belum bayar · dikunci (unpaid, locked).
  3. Click Buka to open a material.
PDFs and videos play inside the app and can't be downloaded. Each material is available until its expiry date ("Luput").

5. Joining a class

  1. Open Kelas saya. Upcoming sessions are listed under Sesi akan datang.
  2. The Sertai (join) button opens 15 minutes before the session starts and stays open until it ends.
  3. Click Sertai. The app checks your access and takes you into the live class.
Always join through the Sertai button in the app. You can only join sessions for your own class, and only when the current month's fee has been recorded.

6. Taking an exam

  1. Open the exam from Ujian Aktif on Utama, or from your materials. The top shows the time limit, the number of questions and your attempts used.
  2. Click Mula ujian. The timer starts. When time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically.
  3. Work through each section (Sebelum / Seterusnya). You can flag questions to come back to.
  4. When you move to the next section, the current one is locked and you can't go back.
  5. Essay questions need you to reach the word target before moving on.
  6. Click Hantar jawapan to submit. You can't change answers afterwards.

Results (Keputusan): multiple-choice answers are marked straight away. Written and essay answers show "Menunggu semakan guru" until your teacher marks them. Exams give you a score; there is no pass or fail mark. Use Cetak keputusan to print. If attempts remain, click Cuba lagi to try again.

Course quizzes (under My Courses) work differently: they have a pass mark, 70% by default.

7. Watching recordings

  1. Open Rakaman.
  2. Click Tonton to watch. Each recording shows the recording date and when it expires.
Recordings are stream-only and can't be downloaded. A recording shows Dikunci (locked) if that month's fee hasn't been recorded.

8. Learning path and progress

  1. Open Laluan belajar and choose a pathway, chapter and lesson.
  2. Each lesson shows Selesai (done), Sambung (continue) or Belum (not started). Use Sambung belajar to pick up where you left off and keep your streak.
  3. Click Lihat kemajuan to see your mastery level for a lesson.

9. Subscribe to a plan

Some platforms also offer subscription plans for course, quiz and exam access. This is separate from your monthly tuition fee, which your administrator records. If you don't see Billing or My Courses in your menu, plans aren't offered on your platform.

  1. Open the plans page (Browse plans under Billing).
  2. Choose a plan and pick Monthly or Annual. The price is shown in RM.
  3. Continue to checkout and complete the payment.
  4. Check the status: Access active means you're set. Activation pending means content stays locked until the payment is confirmed.
  5. Under Billing you can see your paid-through date, download invoices, or choose Cancel at period end.
